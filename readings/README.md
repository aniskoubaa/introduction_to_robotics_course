# Readings

Organised by topic block, matching the week folders in
`../04-copies-of-teaching-material/`. Each topic folder carries a `README.md` naming the
chapters and papers, and why each matters.

| Folder | Weeks | Core reading |
|---|---|---|
| `topic01_foundations_and_ros2` | 1–3 | Official ROS 2 documentation (concepts, tutorials); Corke Ch. 1 |
| `topic02_kinematics_and_control` | 4–5 | Siegwart Ch. 3; Corke Ch. 4 |
| `topic03_perception` | 6 | Siegwart Ch. 4; Corke Ch. 11 |
| `topic04_modelling_and_simulation` | 7–8 | Lynch & Park Ch. 3 (rigid-body motions); URDF/Xacro documentation |
| `topic05_estimation_and_slam` | 9–10 | Thrun Ch. 2–3 (Bayes and Kalman filters), Ch. 8 (MCL); `slam_toolbox` paper |
| `topic06_planning_and_navigation` | 11 | LaValle Ch. 2 (discrete planning); the Nav2 paper (Macenski et al., 2020) |
| `topic07_advanced` | 12 | MoveIt 2 documentation, *or* RT-2 / OpenVLA — depends on the topic chosen |
| `topic08_ethics_and_safety` | 13 | ISO 10218 / ISO 13482 overviews; IEEE Ethically Aligned Design; case studies |

## Essential references

- P. Corke, *Robotics, Vision and Control: Fundamental Algorithms in Python*, 3rd ed., Springer, 2023.
- **Official ROS 2 documentation** (Jazzy) — `docs.ros.org` — a primary, examinable source.

## Supportive

- A. Kelly, *Mobile Robotics: Mathematics, Models and Methods*, Cambridge, 2013.
- K. Lynch and F. Park, *Modern Robotics*, Cambridge, 2017.
- S. Thrun, W. Burgard, D. Fox, *Probabilistic Robotics*, MIT Press, 2005.
- R. Siegwart, I. Nourbakhsh, D. Scaramuzza, *Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots*, 2nd ed., MIT Press, 2011.
- N. Correll, B. Hayes, C. Heckman, A. Roncone, *Introduction to Autonomous Robots*, MIT Press, 2022.
- F. Martins et al., *A Concise Introduction to Robot Programming with ROS 2*, CRC Press, 2022.
