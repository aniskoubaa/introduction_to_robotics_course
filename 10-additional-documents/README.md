## Design precedent

The ROS 2-centred model is adapted from **CS460 — Introduction to Mobile Robots**, Prince
Sultan University (Prof. Anis Koubaa, lead instructor), reduced to a lighter technical load
for a fourth-year EE elective. CS460 used 12 taught weeks with a 4-hour contact block
(3 lecture + 1 tutorial); EE 414 fits 11 taught weeks into 3 hours with no separate tutorial,
so the ROS 1 history, the manipulation block and the deeper control theory are dropped or
compressed to a single advanced slot in Week 12.
